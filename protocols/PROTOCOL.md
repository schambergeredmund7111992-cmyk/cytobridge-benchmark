## Scientific question

The primary question is whether a predictor preserves held-out compound identity after
conditioning on cellular context. CytoBridge is one evaluated predictor, not the benchmark's
definition. The conditional-rank endpoint is an explicit instantiation of Systema centroid
accuracy and the complement of PerturBench transposed rank (up to the registered half-credit
tie rule); it is not claimed as a new metric family.

## Dataset construction

### sci-Plex

Eligible compounds have a valid canonical SMILES, measurements at 10 uM and 24 h, at least
50 treated cells in each of A549, K562, and MCF7, and matching vehicle controls. Eligibility
must not depend on response magnitude. Duplicate compounds with the same canonical SMILES
remain in one split.

The primary `drug_disjoint_v2` split assigns canonical-SMILES groups 70/15/15 percent to
train/validation/test with seed `20260710`. The secondary `scaffold_disjoint_v2` split assigns
Bemis-Murcko scaffold groups with the same fractions and seed. Compounds without a nonempty
Murcko scaffold remain eligible and are conservatively grouped together as `__ACYCLIC__`.
Hyperparameters are selected
only on the primary validation split and are reused unchanged for the scaffold stress test.
The historical nine-drug panel is descriptive legacy analysis only.

Cell QC is applied without labels. Gene filtering, normalization parameters, highly variable
gene selection, and the response-gene panel are fit on training cells/pseudobulks only, then
applied unchanged to validation and test data. Split hashes and ordered gene identifiers are
written before model fitting.

Within each context and acquisition batch, vehicle cells are deterministically divided into
non-overlapping model-input and truth-reference pools with seed `1729`. A prediction may use only
the input-reference pool; target log-fold-change uses only the truth-reference pool. Technical
split-half analyses create independent A/B treated and A/B vehicle pools and never share the
vehicle reference between halves.

Pathway labels use the official Broad `h.all.v2024.1.Hs.symbols.gmt` file (48,690 bytes,
SHA-256 `ee2463540042078bfa3f67828e1e223bb354446d9fbb4d22845866835ba5c772`).
An earlier local copy with `na` in the description column had identical 50 pathway names, gene
order, and membership, but the official file identity is the frozen provenance source.

### Tahoe-100M

Use official revision `2dc5790` metadata plus bounded streaming; do not download the full 1.69 TB
unpacked corpus. Scan `obs_metadata.parquet` in two bounded-memory passes, then stream selected
raw expression rows. Select up to 120 compounds with RDKit-valid canonical SMILES
and a complete matrix across the ten best-covered cell lines. Match `DMSO_TF` controls within
plate. Cap sampling at 64 cells per drug/cell-line/dose/sample. The highest available dose tier
is primary; lower tiers are sensitivity analyses. Preserve `sample` and `plate` as technical
replicate fields. Apply the same 70/15/15 seed-`20260710` drug split and seed-`1729` disjoint
input/truth vehicle pools. Align counts to the frozen sci-Plex gene order, inserting zero columns
for absent genes; stop if overlap is below 90%. Derive Tahoe scoring panels from Tahoe training
targets only.

## Endpoints

The primary endpoint is drug-macro conditional centroid accuracy using Pearson correlation
distance within context on a context-specific top-500 response-gene panel. Each panel is ranked
by mean absolute training log-fold-change and is frozen before validation/test scoring. For an
anchor prediction, every same-context wrong compound is a negative: a correct match earns 1,
a tie earns 0.5, and a wrong ordering earns 0. Conditional transposed rank is `1 - accuracy`.
Report both names and the matched-minus-mismatched correlation gap.

The main secondary endpoint is pair-own Spearman correlation on the 50 genes with largest
absolute truth for that pair. It must never be computed on a union of test-pair top genes.
Top-k boundary ties are resolved by frozen gene order; rank ties use average ranks. Constant
predictions score zero, while non-finite inputs or constant truth on the scored panel stop the
analysis as protocol failures.
Other secondary endpoints are all-gene Pearson, inter-drug prediction correlation, and response
magnitude error. The mean baseline is computed from training targets only, separately by
context. Random and oracle controls must score near chance and ceiling, respectively. Before any
model result is released, Random conditional accuracy must lie in `[0.40, 0.60]` and Oracle must
be at least `0.99`; failure stops the affected benchmark rather than changing either bound.

## Models and selection

On sci-Plex, compare Random, Oracle, training Mean, Morgan-2048 Ridge, official-architecture
`chemCPA (from scratch)`,
official biolord, and CytoBridge under one split, gene order, target construction, and artifact
format. On Tahoe, compare Random, Oracle, training Mean, Ridge, and CytoBridge. External-model
outputs are re-aggregated by the benchmark pipeline; model-specific reconstructions cannot enter
the primary leaderboard.

Use seeds `[11, 23, 42, 67, 101]`. Screening uses seed 42 and the frozen budgets in
`sweep_spec.yaml`. Choose by validation conditional accuracy, with pair-own Spearman as the
sole tie-breaker. Test labels remain untouched until a configuration is frozen. The scaffold
split receives no retuning.

After screening, freeze both hyperparameters and the selected epoch count. Final learned models
are refit on train+validation for exactly that many epochs, with no validation pass, checkpoint
selection, or early stopping. Ridge selects alpha on training/validation, then refits on their
union. Official external models follow the same selection/refit boundary.

CytoBridge screening runs for at most 20 epochs, evaluates every epoch, and stops after five
validation evaluations without improvement. chemCPA runs from scratch for exactly 201 epochs and
evaluates the shared validation endpoints after epochs 50, 100, 150, 200, and 201. It uses the
official pinned architecture and training implementation, training-derived RDKit descriptors,
and no pretrained or previously sci-Plex-fitted weights. biolord runs for at most 500
epochs, evaluates every 20 epochs, and stops after 20 shared validation evaluations without
improvement. These different budgets preserve the official training regimes; every model is
still selected by the same registered validation endpoint.

CytoBridge ablations are: full; `lam_recon=0` with direct logFC supervision; per-gene normalized
reconstruction; no pathway gate; and drug-blind/no MolFormer. All use five seeds. Record gradient
norms for active losses. Encoders remain precomputed/frozen; the paper will not make a causal
claim that freezing caused any observed failure.

### Frozen numerical and loading gate

Before screening, run the same seed, initialization, and first frozen batch for two optimizer
steps under `32-true` and `bf16-mixed`. Every model output, raw/weighted loss, individual
gradient value, gradient norm, and post-step parameter must be finite. fp32 is mandatory; use
bf16 only when both precisions pass. If fp32 passes and bf16 fails, all formal CytoBridge runs
use `32-true`. If fp32 fails, stop before training. The gate JSON hash is a required source
input, and this pre-execution branch is not a post-result retry.

Training workers use a non-fork multiprocessing context. Select `num_workers` only from
`[0, 2, 4, 8]` using label-free 200-batch timing; a candidate must reproduce the worker-zero
anchor and hard-negative index stream exactly. Freeze the fastest passing candidate in the
runtime report. Worker count, pinning, and prefetching may change throughput only and may not
change sample order, batch size, loss, or optimizer semantics.

### Frozen scGPT inference

Use scGPT 0.2.4 and checkpoint files with the SHA-256 values recorded in
`handoff/STATUS.json`. Construct the transformer from `args.json`; every tensor used by
`TransformerModel._encode` must have exact checkpoint key and shape coverage. Filter genes absent
from the vocabulary, retain nonzero values, prepend `<cls>` with value `-2`, apply the official
51-bin quantile routine, and cap the sequence at the checkpoint length of 1200. Truncation keeps
`<cls>` and samples the remaining tokens with a deterministic per-cell seed derived from
`20260710`, row position, cell identifier, and purpose. Padding uses `<pad>`/`-2` and is masked.

Cache the L2-normalized `<cls>` state only, as float32 `[N, 1, 512]`, using batch size 32 and fp32
inference. The source matrix is the leakage-safe protocol-specific `sciplex_processed.h5ad` `X`
(training-HVG, normalized log1p expression). Each cache requires its generated provenance JSON
and a passing `scripts/validate_scgpt_cache.py` report; mean pooling, full-token caches, unknown
gene-to-pad mapping, checkpoint substitution, or post-result preprocessing changes are forbidden.

MolFormer tokens use `ibm/MoLFormer-XL-both-10pct` at revision
`a14249e5ad9e3e7c3b1bb604393e914cfcebd2c8`, length 128, and float32 output. Each protocol
cache must carry provenance binding the source SMILES table, model revision, shape, finiteness,
and distinct per-drug token rows.

chemCPA and biolord consume the shared `log1p(raw counts)` AnnData export containing only
input-reference controls. Their row-aligned output is clipped at zero in log-expression space,
mapped back with `expm1`, and passed through the same pseudobulk/logFC implementation as every
other model. The clipped fraction is reported; truth reconstruction must match the frozen target
numerically before an artifact is accepted.

## Inference and multiplicity

The reporting unit is held-out drug. Report per-drug means across contexts, then the drug-macro
mean. Use 10,000 drug-cluster bootstrap draws for confidence intervals. For multi-seed estimates,
resample seeds and drugs hierarchically while preserving model pairing. Permutation tests apply
one drug-label permutation consistently across every context in the balanced evaluation panel.
Secondary tests use Benjamini-Hochberg FDR. There is no post-hoc AUC threshold.

## Stop conditions

Stop the affected run immediately for NaN/Inf loss, suspected split or feature leakage,
impossible control values, baseline-protocol mismatch, or code-paper inconsistency. Record the
event in `experiments/<tag>/POSTMORTEM.md`; do not silently retry or substitute a protocol.
